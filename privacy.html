Privacy

Last updated 1 November 2026.

This site is a static marketing page for The Unofficial Grok Bot Starter Guide, sold by Trevor Aquino, United States.

This website

The site does not set cookies and does not load analytics, ads, or other trackers. It is static files. Your browser may still send a normal request log to GitHub Pages when you load a page. I do not run a separate analytics product on top of that.

Purchases

Checkout is handled by Stripe, which processes your payment data. After payment you are taken straight to the PDF download page. Separately, an automatic email from taquino-inbox@agentmail.to delivers the same download link. This site does not collect or store card numbers, bank details, or checkout forms.

Email

Stripe uses the email address you give at checkout to process your payment. I use that same address to send your download link, a revised file if I publish one, and to handle support or sales-policy questions. I do not sell it or use it for unrelated newsletters.

If you email taquino-inbox@agentmail.to, I keep that thread long enough to help you and to keep a record of the delivery issue.

What I do not collect on this site

  • No account sign-up.
  • No newsletter form.
  • No advertising pixels.
  • No payment fields hosted on these pages.
