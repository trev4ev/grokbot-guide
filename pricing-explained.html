Grok Bot pricing explained

Figures below are from the official pricing page, checked October 31, 2026. Prices are monthly, exclude tax, and change over time, so confirm the current numbers on x.ai/pricing before you pay.

Grok Bot Plus vs Premium

This is the comparison most beginners actually need. Plus, at $8 a month, covers general chat and agent use from a baseline shared pool, but it does not include Grok Bot at all. Premium, at $30 a month, is the first tier that does include it, along with about four times the usage pool of Plus. Premium is also the plan the official pricing page points new AI users toward.

In practice, that means staying on Plus will not get you Grok Bot no matter how long you wait or what settings you check. If Grok Bot is the reason you are looking at plans, Premium is the entry point, not Plus.

How Grok Bot usage limits work

Usage comes out of a shared weekly pool rather than a per-bot allowance, so running three bots draws from the same pool as running one. If you use up the pool, top-ups are available to add more usage without changing your plan.

A free trial is handled as a usage credit with a 7-day window. Once that window closes, unused credit is not restored, so it is worth actually testing Grok Bot inside the trial period rather than starting late in the week.

Team seats and legacy plans

Grok Bot access is not limited to one individual signup path. A team seat on an eligible tier works the same way as an individual seat for access purposes, so if your plan is managed through a workplace or shared billing account, check with whoever administers that seat rather than assuming you are stuck on whatever the default looks like from your side.

The eligibility rules also cover legacy and linked plans, meaning an older plan name or a plan tied to another product can still qualify if it maps to one of the tiers above. If your account shows a plan name that does not match Free, Plus, Premium, Super, or Ultra exactly, that is a reason to check your account settings or billing page directly instead of guessing from this table.
